01 / The gap

Most models are graded on a snapshot. Molecules live on a landscape.

Force MAE matters, but it hides the mistakes that alter which molecular shapes are visited and how often a system crosses between them. KinetiGuard puts those kinetic questions in the experiment plan from day one.

02 / The method

Three signals. One honest decision.

Each part has a simple job, and each has a visible failure mode.

A

Conservative 3D potential

The model predicts an energy, then obtains forces as its coordinate gradient. That keeps the physics internally consistent.

F(x) = −∇E(x)
B

Calibrated uncertainty

A small ensemble disagrees where it has not learned enough. A held-out set turns that disagreement into a coverage-tested force bound.

bound(x) = q · spread(x)
C

Kinetic-risk acquisition

When uncertainty coincides with negative curvature, the system flags a potentially transition-relevant region for the next expensive label.

risk = bound × transition weight

05 / Claim boundary

A strong demo should make the limits easy to see.

KinetiGuard is not a drug-discovery engine and this page does not invent benchmark wins. The right claim today is simple: the research system, GPU path, and evaluation discipline are real; the chemistry results arrive only after the 3BPA and kinetic-network experiments finish.
